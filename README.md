# Day 1 - CRM Basics

## 1. What is CRM?

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a system used by organizations to manage customer information, track interactions, and improve customer relationships.

## 2. Why Companies Use Salesforce

* Stores customer data in one place
* Tracks sales opportunities
* Improves communication with customers
* Generates reports and dashboards
* Automates business processes

## 3. Salesforce Objects

### Account

An Account represents a company or organization that a business works with.

### Contact

A Contact is an individual person associated with an Account.

### Opportunity

An Opportunity represents a potential sale or business deal.
